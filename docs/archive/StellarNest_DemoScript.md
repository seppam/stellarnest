## VOICEOVER TIPS

- **Read the bracketed bold tags as EMPHASIS** — those words should be slightly louder or slower
- **[On screen:]** lines = what to show visually, not speak
- **[MUSIC:]** = sound cues for editing — not spoken
- Target pace: ~130 words per minute (natural, conversational — not rushed)
- Record each section separately so you can re-record without re-doing everything
- Keep a consistent ~6 inch distance from mic for even audio levels
